ML Kit

Face detection and text recognition are provided by Google ML Kit
(com.google.mlkit:face-detection, com.google.mlkit:text-recognition).
The images are processed on the device and never leave it.

ML Kit is licensed under the ML Kit Terms of Service and the Google APIs
Terms of Service:

https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms
https://developers.google.com/terms

The libraries bundle open source software. The notices for that software are
part of the libraries (third_party_licenses) and are listed at:

https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/license
